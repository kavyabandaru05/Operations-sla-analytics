# Executive Summary – Operations SLA Analytics

## 1. Project Overview

This project analyzes support ticket operations to evaluate SLA compliance, ticket resolution performance, team efficiency, root causes of SLA breaches, and customer satisfaction.

The analysis uses a simulated support ticket dataset covering July–December 2025.

## 2. Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Tickets | 6,656 |
| SLA Breach Rate | 36.09% |
| Average Resolution Time | 29.16 hours |
| Average First Response Time | 3.78 hours |
| Backlog | 146 |
| Average CSAT | 3.77 / 5 |
| Reopened Ticket Rate | 5.86% |

## 3. Key Findings

1. **SLA Compliance:** 36.09% of tickets breached their SLA targets, indicating an opportunity to improve resolution efficiency.

2. **Team Performance:** Logistics recorded the highest SLA breach rate at 55.14%, followed by Billing at 48.05%.

3. **Monthly Trend:** December recorded the highest breach rate at 41.45%.

4. **Root Cause:** Performance issues contributed the highest number of SLA breaches, with 228 breached tickets.

5. **Customer Satisfaction:** Tickets without SLA breaches had an average CSAT of 4.07, compared with 3.24 for breached tickets.

## 4. Business Recommendations

- Review workload distribution and resolution workflows in the Logistics and Billing teams.
- Investigate recurring performance issues and prioritize preventive maintenance.
- Introduce early-warning alerts for tickets approaching SLA deadlines.
- Monitor high-volume months and plan staffing capacity accordingly.
- Improve communication with customers when ticket resolution is delayed.
- Track SLA compliance and CSAT together to monitor service quality.

## 5. Conclusion

The analysis identifies opportunities to improve SLA compliance, reduce resolution delays, and enhance customer satisfaction. The interactive Power BI dashboard enables stakeholders to explore performance by month, team, priority, channel, and customer tier.

*Note: Findings are based on simulated data and should not be interpreted as actual company performance.*
